# Innate Traits of a Content

Our intention is use NLP techniques to extract all the innate traits. 

User will also have the right to assign these traits to their posts manually if they want. 

## 1. Topic

Search for from existing cluster labels of lv1 lvl2 lvl3 topics (all three unionised)

Write your own topic (smaller the better)

Serves a similar purpose to hashtags in twitter. (when user writes a hashtag, we suggest these topics with a topics window; they write anything they want, but joining and existing cluster increases visibility). 

## 2. Stance

1. For
2. Against
3. Neutral 
4. Mixed

## 3. Type of Information Provided

1. Background info
2. Present, additional info
3. Future info

## 4. Basis of the Content

1. Evidence based
2. Reasoning / opinion based (conscious reasoning, sub conscious intuition) 

## 5. Sentiment

(less important)

1. Positive
2. Negative
3. Neutral 
4. Mixed

# 

## 

A content (comment, reply, link) present in the underpost region will have the above 5 of the labels.  Different combinations of these 5 labels covers broad classes from the [Labels of a Related Content](Labels%20of%20a%20Related%20Content%202ddf88751ac3800abdc8f3bba1156373.md) 

Users can choose these all the 5 labels when commenting and replying (not compulsory but will rank better if they do).

And choose 1st, 4th and 5th label while posting (not as comment or a reply). 

It is not compulsory to choose any of these labels but they increase the visibility on the platform. 

Decoder LLMs can be used for high impact lvl2 clusters to create [Labels of a Related Content](Labels%20of%20a%20Related%20Content%202ddf88751ac3800abdc8f3bba1156373.md)
