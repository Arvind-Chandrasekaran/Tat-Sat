# Solves a Problem

We validated Tat Sat’s ability to serve as a valuable platform for news discourse through early user testing and structured interviews conducted after we implemented at first cycle of the recommendation algorithm.

### User interviews Details

We conducted in-person and remote sessions to emulate different consumption behaviors. In total, we tested with ~25 users, segmented as follows:

- Social-media–first consumers (primarily Twitter/X and Instagram)
- Discussion-driven users who rely heavily on Reddit
- Video–driven users (YouTube, TikTok)
- Non–social-media users (primarily older users) who rely on traditional digital news outlets such as BBC, Fox News, and similar platforms

Each segment had at least 6 participants, allowing us to observe consistent patterns across consumption styles rather than feedback from a single user type.

### Prototype tested

Users interacted with an early prototype that implemented:

- The core recommendation logic (topic-based recommendation with graph algorithms)
- Contextual augmentation (related posts, background information obtained through NLP techniques)

### Evaluation framework

After using the prototype, users rated their experience across five dimensions (out of 10):

1. Recommendation relevance
2. Information absorption (how much meaningful news they could absorb in a fixed time window)
3. Cognitive load (mental effort required to consume content)
4. Willingness to use the product regularly if launched with broader data coverage

### Results

Across all user segments, scores were consistently high:

- Recommendation relevance: 9 / 10
- Information absorption: 8.2 / 10
- Low cognitive load: 8.9 / 10
- 
    - Notably, even users who primarily relied on short-form video platforms reported that the prototype helped them understand news faster and with less mental fatigue compared to their existing platforms.
- Willingness to adopt: ~90% of users said they would use the product daily if it launched with real-time news discourses.
