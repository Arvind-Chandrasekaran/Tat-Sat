# Recommendation Pipeline:

To explain the pipeline we take the example of an avg indian in his early 30s who works as an auditor for a small company. He likes to consume simple media related what his colleqgues and friends do and discusses on these media. He lives in Salem.

1. Select by Relevancy
    1. By **content-topic**
        1. **Consumer** follows the **content-topic** (Ex- **consumer** wants to consume the **content** related to the **topic** 'IPL Auction' and follows it.)
        2. **Consumer** does not follow the **content-topic** but wants its **content**. (Ex - **consumer** would want to consume the **content** related to the **topics** 'IPL Auction for CSK', 'South Africa Tour of India' and 'Tamil Movies'. ) (Twitter is mainly a news platform and it does not suggest a completely new topic, youtube does that because it is learning platform. Be careful with suggesting completely new topics - if already likes IPL Auction then suggesting Indian vs SA is fine but suggesting Tamil Movies is a bit risky) (give option for this)
            
            **Consumer** can follow broad topics like American Politics, Indian Politics and specific topics like Bihar Elections within Indian politics or chirag paswan within bihar electtions. If someone only follows indian politics, they should still receive trump tweets that talk about tariffs on india (so topic modelling needs to be done carefully)
            
    2. By **creator**
        1. **Consumer** follows an **creator** (Ex - **consumer** wants to consume the **content** created by the **creator** 'Ashwin' and follows him)
        2. **Consumer** does not follow an **creator** but wants their **content** (Ex **consumer** would want to consume the content created by **creators** Harsha Bhogle and Aakash Chopra but does not follow them. 'Would want' is measured by relevance score based similarity between **creator-topic** and **consumer-topic)** (**creator-topic** and **consumer-topic** are multi-dimensional representation of different topics of content made by an creator)
    
    Ex- In this case, the **consumer** would want to see any **content** by ashwin whether relevant or not to cricket or not since he follows them but would not want to non-cricketing **content** by creators he does not follow like Akash Chopra. But we prevent this, since this is against quality discourse. So if Ashwin makes non cricketing **content,** it will not rank high simply because he has so many followers. (this also reduces promo tweets, which is an incentive for **creators.**) (BTS fan would want to know anything BTS is doing music related or not).  (ranking should look after this)
    
2. Rank by Relevancy
    1. By **date-timestamp**
        1. New **content** (Ex - **consumer** wants to consume new **content** by Manjarekar in IPL Auction 2026 and not IPL auction 2024.) (ranked much higher)
        2. Old **content** but now relevant (Ex **consumer** would want consume content where Manjarekar says that a Cam Green does not a bright future in t20 in 2022, which now becomes relevant as he is expected get some of the highest bids in IPL Auction 2026. This **content** can be presented as post/comment/reply the **main window**, or as **link** within a **content window.**) (
    2. By creator-demographics
